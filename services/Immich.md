Immich is basically used as a way to backup my precious photos and videos. 

The 3-2-1 rule of data protection is lowkey applied here: each photo has 3 copies, one on the remote devices, one on the Immich storage, and one on an external hard drive copied from the storage manually. 

Access model:
- Internal access via LAN
- Remote access via Tailscale

As of right now, Immich is using the own server's storage. I will change that to a dedicated dataset once I have my hands on a NAS machine.
